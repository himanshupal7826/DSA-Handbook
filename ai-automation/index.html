Every chapter follows the same 10 steps: overview, key concepts, setup, runnable Python 3.11+ code, a real business use case, a 1–2 hour mini project, a comparison table, mistakes & best practices, exercises with self-check questions, and links to the official docs. Free and open-source first (Ollama, Chroma, self-hosted n8n); paid options are named alongside. For the theory underneath (transformers, training, fine-tuning) see the AI Engineering handbook.
